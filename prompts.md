# Prompt e ficha técnica

## Prompt de geração

> A candid documentary photograph, landscape 3:2, of a fictional small electronics repair bench in Florianópolis just after a rainstorm. Viewed from seated eye level. On the bench: one worn compact digital multimeter with screen turned off, two loose probes with realistically draped leads, a small screwdriver, an open unmarked notebook with blank pages, and a partly finished cup of coffee in a plain ceramic mug. Side window with soft rain droplets, late-afternoon muted light, subtle film grain, authentic modest Brazilian workshop, small signs of use, restrained colors of charcoal, slate and amber. Physically plausible cast shadows and reflections. 35 mm photojournalism, no people, no readable text, no logos, no watermarks, no dramatic grading.
